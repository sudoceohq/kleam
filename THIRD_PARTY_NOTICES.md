(see [LICENSE](LICENSE)). The banks are generated by
[`macos/scripts/synthesize_switches.py`](macos/scripts/synthesize_switches.py).

No third-party recordings are bundled. In particular Kleam does not ship audio from
Klack, Mechvibes, kbsim, Monkeytype, or community “personal use” packs.

[`macos/scripts/import_mit_packs.py`](macos/scripts/import_mit_packs.py) can fetch
MIT-licensed Mechvibes and tplai recordings. It is **unused**. Running it would
replace synth banks and would require keeping those MIT notices. Do not run it
unless the project decides to vendor that audio again.
